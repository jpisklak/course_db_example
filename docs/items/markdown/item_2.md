### Question 
$\Phi$ is a very sacred number called the "Golden Ratio". Some people believe that it has magick properties which allow us, mere mortals, to understand more than just the natural world. It is approximately equal to:

1.6180339887498948482045868343656381177203091798057

Including the 1 preceding the decimal, what is the mode of the first 21 digits of $\Phi$?

### Correct Answer
8

### Solution:
Counting the frequency of each digit, the most frequently occurring digit is 8, therefore the mode is 8.
