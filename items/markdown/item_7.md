### Question 

The mean of two numbers is 639.5, and one number is 37 more than the other. What is the value of the smaller of the two numbers?



### Correct Answer
621

### Solution:
We can solve this with some basic algebra where $x$ is the smaller of the two numbers:

$$
\begin{align}
\frac{x + (x + 37)}{2} &= 639.5 \\[0.5em]
\frac{2x + 37}{2}      &= 639.5 \\[0.5em]
2x + 37                &= 639.5 \times 2 \\[0.5em]
2x                            &= (639.5 \times 2) - 37 \\[0.5em]
x                             &= \frac{(639.5 \times 2) - 37}{2} \\[0.5em]
x                             &= 621
\end{align}
$$
