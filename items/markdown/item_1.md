### Question 
A student scored the following percentages on their end of the year exams:

* Alchemy: 82
* Astrology: 87
* Falconry: 78
* Logic: 89
* Music: 57
* Swordsmanship: 64
* Tarot: 81
* Xylomancy: 98

What was their mean score?

### Correct Answer
79.5

### Solution:
The mean score is calculated as:

$$\frac{\sum x}{N} = \frac{82+87+78+89+57+64+81+98}{8} = \frac{636}{8} = 79.5$$

In R:
```r
x <- c(82, 87, 78, 89, 57, 64, 81, 98)
mean(x)
```

The mean score is **79.50**.
